Stolen Realm Mods

Seventeen small quality-of-life mods for our group. Nothing changes for players who don't install them, and nothing is sent to anyone else.

Install (2 minutes)

Easiest: run Install Stolen Realm Mods.exe. It finds the game, installs the shared settings, and checks GitHub every time you run it, so running the same exe again later installs the newest pack. Latest download: github.com/Kalarian/StolenRealm-Mods/releases. By hand:

  1. In Steam, right-click Stolen Realm → Manage → Browse local files.
  2. Extract the zip into that folder, so winhttp.dll and the BepInEx folder sit next to Stolen Realm.exe. If Windows asks to merge folders, say yes.
  3. Launch the game as normal. That's it. To uninstall, delete winhttp.dll and the BepInEx folder.

The following pages describe each mod and its settings in detail.

Better loot

DropRates · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.droprates.cfg

Reshapes what enemies drop so that a run produces less grey and green clutter and noticeably more rare, legendary and mythic gear. It changes the chance of each rarity per kill, the best rarity each kind of enemy may drop, and how often named "personal" drops (a boss's signature item) appear. The Merchant's equipment slot and Ulf's gambling table are also tilted toward the top rarities. Roguelike mode is left exactly as the game ships it.

As shipped: per kill, greys drop a quarter as often as normal and greens a bit less often; rares four times as often; legendaries about 8% of kills (capped at 16% even with Endless bonuses); mythics about 12% of eligible kills (capped at 25%). Soldiers can now drop rares, elites legendaries, champions mythics. Named boss/elite drops are five times as likely. Enemies summoned by other enemies (totems, props, clones) keep the normal rolls, so a boss that spawns thirty props does not hand out thirty mythic rolls. The town armorer and jeweler stock rare or better, with legendary and mythic odds that climb each act; gold is the limit there, not availability.

XP that scales with difficulty

DifficultyXP · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.difficultyxp.cfg

The game pays more gold on harder difficulties but the same experience. This mod makes experience follow the same curve as gold: Classic +25%, Veteran +50%, Torturous +75%, Heart of the Realm +100%, Endless +25%. It applies to battle experience, quest-completion experience and, by default, island events that grant experience (which the game never scaled). Temporary gold bonuses on your character, such as the campfire "Prepared" buff, are mirrored into experience the same way. Roguelike is not affected, since it levels you per battle rather than by experience.

Cheaper upgrades

QoL · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.qol.cfg

Noor's Materials normally charges the full shop price of the item at its new level, times 1.5, no matter how far behind the item is. So upgrading a piece every couple of levels costs far more overall than one big jump. With this mod the price is the difference between the item's current level and the target level, times the same 1.5, so topping up regularly costs the same in total as waiting.

Settings

"What will this click do?"

TargetTooltip · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.targettooltip.cfg

In battle, hovering an enemy with the attack cursor, or any valid cell with a skill selected, shows a tooltip for the action a click would fire: the basic attack the game will use, or the selected skill. Under it, when the cell holds an enemy, a preview line reads something like vs Goblin Archer: 71-96 (45-60% of its health). That is the game's own damage calculation for that exact target, after its resistances, armor, damage reduction and any bonuses you have against it, so you can see before committing whether the hit will finish it. A smaller detail line shows the split by damage type and what reduced it. If the cell has a ground effect (burning ground, poison cloud), its description is folded into the same tooltip under "On this tile" instead of replacing it.

Hotkey: F10 flips between the Full style (the game's normal skill tooltip with damage, cost, cooldown and status) and the Compact style (icon and name in a small translucent box). The choice is saved.

Enemy specials explained

SpecialTooltips · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.specialtooltips.cfg

Right-click an enemy to open the Examine window. The entries under Passive already had tooltips; the ones under Special (Armored, Unpredictable, Pack Hunter, Undying, Explosive, Volatile and about ninety others) did not, and many of the names are not self-explanatory. With this mod each Special entry can be hovered and shows a plain-language explanation. Skill scaling shown

ScalingTooltips · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.scalingtooltips.cfg

Every damage or healing number in a skill, action or status tooltip gets a small grey label saying where it comes from, for example 118-159 (0.7x AP): this skill deals 0.7 times your Attack Power. AP is Attack Power, which comes from your weapon; SP is Spell Power. When the formula names a damage type it is added too, e.g. (0.5x SP, Fire). This works in the skill trees while you are choosing what to learn, on the action bar, in Examine and in text links, so you can tell at a glance that Garrote (0.8x) hits harder per point than Fracture (0.6x).

Below the description a Scaling: line shows the whole chain for your character, e.g. Scaling: 0.7 x 142 AP x 1.33 = 132 (112-152) · Power +12%, Physical +21%: the skill's factor, your current stat, every percentage bonus you have (skill power, damage type bonuses from gear, mana power, basic-attack bonus, Might), any flat damage, and the result with the game's normal damage spread. Bonuses at zero are left out.

Fortunes shared across your characters

SharedFortunes · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.sharedfortunes.cfg

Every fortune any of your characters has ever earned becomes available to all of them, so a new character does not have to farm the same island events again. The mod keeps a pool of every fortune your characters own and the highest level each was earned at (a small file next to your character saves). When a character loads, fortunes they are missing are added to their available list, unequipped, and fortunes they own at a lower level are raised. Which fortunes are slotted stays a per-character choice.

  • Nothing is ever removed or lowered. A friend installing this mod keeps every fortune they already had; their own fortunes simply start their pool.
  • By default a shared fortune is capped at the receiving character's level and grows with them; a level-10 character gets a level-18 fortune at level 10, then 11, 12 and so on as they level.
  • Earning a fortune you already own at the same or a lower level changes nothing; at a higher level it raises it, for every character.
  • Fortune upgrades bought with the Fortune upgrades mod count as earned and spread to your other characters the same way.
  • Only your own characters on your own PC are involved. In co-op your friends' fortunes never enter your pool and yours never reach them.

Settings

EnabledtrueShare fortunes across your characters.
CapToCharacterLeveltrueA shared fortune never exceeds the receiving character's level, and rises as they level. Off gives every character the pool's full level immediately.
IncludeRoguelikeCharactersfalseAlso share to and from Roguelike characters.
ReloadKey / VerboseLoggingF9 / trueRe-read this file and rescan all character saves into the pool; log every fortune added or raised.

Fortune upgrades

FortuneUpgrade · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.fortuneupgrade.cfg

Fortunes are earned at the level of the island you found them on and normally stay there. This mod lets you pay gold to raise a fortune to your character's current level, the way you upgrade an item at Noor's. In town, open the Fortune window, hover a fortune and its tooltip shows a line such as Upgrade to L17: 25,920 gold (press U). Press U, confirm, and the fortune is raised and the gold taken from your party. If it cannot be upgraded right now the tooltip says why (already at your level, not in town, in battle).

The price is exactly what a two-handed weapon of the same rarity would cost to upgrade between those levels, times the game's usual 1.5 upgrade markup, and you only pay for the levels gained: raising a fortune from 20 to 30 costs less than from 1 to 30. A mythic fortune taken all the way to level 30 is a serious purchase; a common one is pocket change. With the Fortunes-shared mod installed, an upgrade spreads to your other characters too.

Hotkey: U while hovering a fortune in the Fortune window (in town).

Levels shared across your characters

LevelSync · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.levelsync.cfg

Every character you own is kept at the level of your highest character, so alts are always ready to play alongside your main. When a character loads at a lower level it is raised to the account's highest, with the skill points and stat points that level brings (the game works those out from level, so they simply appear, unspent). Health and mana keep their percentages, exactly as a normal level-up does. While playing, experience earned by one character keeps the rest of your party at the same level after each reward; each member still earns its own share, the sync only tops up whoever ended lower.

  • Levels are never lowered, and nothing goes above the game's cap of 30.
  • A brand-new character is left alone on the creation screen (normal starting points) and raised the first time it enters the game.
  • Roguelike characters are left out by default, since that mode levels you per battle.
  • Only your own characters count. In co-op, a friend's higher-level character never changes yours, and your level never changes theirs.
  • A small file next to your saves shows the current highest level and which character set it.

Settings

EnabledtrueKeep every character at the account's highest level.

Junk auto-sold, materials auto-stored

AutoSalvage · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.autosalvage.cfg

The moment loot lands in your bags (battle loot, chests, event rewards) the rules run on the items that just arrived and a short message appears over your character, e.g. +340 gold, sold 4, stashed 2. You still see the loot in the post-battle list; it just never clutters the bag. The rest of the bag is never judged, so a blue you bought and left unequipped survives the next drop.

  • Equipment (weapons, shields, head, armor, rings, amulets): white, green and blue pieces are sold at the normal shop sell price, each rarity with its own switch. Legendary and mythic pieces are always kept. Optional exception: keep blues at or above your level.
  • Trade commodities are sold, whatever their rarity, except the ones any crafting recipe uses (Animal Hide, Wild Soul, Fire Core, drake scales, Cursed Coin and so on), which go to storage instead so recipes stay craftable.
  • Crafting materials go straight to your storage. They are never sold.
  • Never touched: anything equipped, consumables, tools, quest items, anything you bought at a shop, crafted, won at Ulf's, moved between characters, took out of storage, received from a friend, picked as a Roguelike level-up reward, or a new character's starting kit.
  • One-time cleanup: the first launch tidies what is already in every character's bag (unequipped items only), then turns that setting off by itself. Set it to true again to repeat it.
  • Roguelike mode and Roguelike characters are left alone.

Threat overlay

ThreatOverlay · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.threatoverlay.cfg

In battle, hold Left Alt and the map shows what the enemies can do on their next turn. Every hex an enemy can move to is tinted red, using the game's own movement rules (its movement points, terrain and height, blocked hexes, slowing or blocking ground effects; a rooted enemy gets no red at all). Every hex an enemy could attack from any of those positions is tinted orange, using its longest attack range: weapon range for melee, the longest harmful skill for casters and archers. Release the key and the map goes back to normal, with your own movement and targeting highlights intact.

Use it before committing a move: a hex with no tint is one nobody can reach or hit next turn, an orange hex means somebody can hit you there, and a red hex is where enemies will be able to stand. Everything is computed on your own PC from what you can already see; nothing is changed in the game and nothing is sent to other players.

Limits: skill ranges are treated as a plain radius (long lines and blast shapes are not drawn), and enemies that teleport or dash are shown by their walking range only.

Hotkey: hold Left Alt in battle. Change it with HoldKey.

Readable numbers

Item skill tooltips

ItemSkillTooltips · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.itemskilltooltips.cfg

An item that grants a skill only tells you the skill's name. Roguelike QoL: better events, rewards and gear

RoguelikeQoL · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.roguelikeqol.cfg

Campaign progress shared across your characters

SharedProgress · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.sharedprogress.cfg

The game keeps campaign progress per character: which quests and towns you have completed, how far into the story you are (which act), the highest quest level you have finished and how good the shops' stock is. With this mod every campaign character has your most advanced character's progress: a fresh character loads into the same town, sees the same quest map with the same quests done and unlocked, can pick the same quest levels and finds the same shop stock.   • Nothing is ever taken away. The mod only adds completed quests and raises numbers; a character ahead of the others is never pulled back.

  • Only your own characters on your own PC. In co-op your friends' progress is never read or changed.
  • A small file next to your saves (SharedProgress.json) holds the combined progress so a deleted character never costs the others anything.

Battle stats that add up

BattleStats · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.battlestats.cfg

After every fight the game's Stats button shows damage dealt, taken, blocked and healed per character. It already counts poison and burn ticks inside Damage Dealt, but it never says where the damage came from, so a nature build whose enemies die walking through poison tiles looks like it did nothing. This mod records every hit while the fight is resolved and adds four sections to that window, in the same style, below the game's own rows, and the table scrolls (mouse wheel, drag, or the slim bar on the right):

  • Damage Breakdown: Direct Hits, Over Time (poison, burn, bleed ticks), Ground Tiles (tiles someone walked into or stood on), Summons, Thorns.
  • Hits: Hits / Crits with the crit rate, Kills / Overkill, Biggest Hit with the skill that did it, Best Skill with its total, Damage Per Turn.
  • Damage By Element: Physical, Fire, Cold, Lightning, Shadow, Holy.
  • Activity: Casts (free actions in brackets), Mana Spent, Hexes Moved, Taken From Ticks.

Hover a character's name at the top of the window for a tooltip with their top damage sources (skill or status, total, number of hits), or hover any damage number in the table to see which abilities made up that number for that character. A kill by a poison tick counts for whoever applied the poison; a summon's damage counts for its master, as the game already does.

Multiplayer: the fight is resolved on the host's PC, so the host needs the mod for anything to be recorded; everyone with the mod then sees the same numbers. If the host has no mod the new rows show n/a. Nothing is changed about the fight itself.

One gold purse for all your characters

SharedGold · settings in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.sharedgold.cfg

All the gold your campaign characters hold is treated as one purse. Whoever you are playing holds all of it, so a solo character or a full party always sees and can spend the whole balance, and gold earned by one character is there for the next. The first time the mod runs it simply gathers what your characters already had; the total is exactly the sum of their purses, nothing gained and nothing lost. Switching characters moves the purse with you.

  • It cannot create or destroy gold. It only moves gold between your own characters, and the sum is preserved by construction. Loot, quest rewards, shop spending, fortune upgrades and sending gold to a friend all work as normal and change the purse by exactly their amount.
  • Roguelike characters are left out entirely. Hardcore characters share the purse unless you switch that off.
  • A party of two of your own characters shows the purse once, not twice.
  • Only your own characters on your own PC are touched. In co-op your friends' gold is never read or changed, and sending gold to them works exactly as before.
  • Every move is written to both characters' save files on the spot and checked; if a file cannot be written the move is undone, so a character you never play that session can never keep a stale purse on disk.
  • Removing the mod leaves the whole purse on whichever character held it last; nothing is lost. A small file next to your saves shows the current total and who holds it.

Turning things off

Every mod has an Enabled line in its own file, and all twelve have a master line in BepInEx\config\stolenrealm.mods.cfg. Change a line to false, press F9 in game, and that mod is completely off until you turn it back on. No restart needed.
