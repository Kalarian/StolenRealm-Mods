BattleStats Test Plan

One battle, six level-18 characters, every kind of damage the stats page should be able to explain. Phase 1 only records; nothing shows in game.

Purpose

The recorder tags every damage, healing and block credit with how it arrived: a direct hit, a status tick (poison, bleed, burn), a tile's on-enter effect, a tile ticking at the start of a turn, a tile firing as it is placed, thorns, or a summon. At the end of the fight it prints each character's totals next to the numbers the game's own stats page uses. The test has one question to answer: does the game already count ticks and tiles for the caster, or does it drop them? Everything else (crits, kills, overkill, biggest hit, per-skill totals) is checked on the way.

Setup

  1. Host the game yourself and bring all six characters in your own party. Recording happens on the machine that resolves the battle, so a solo party of six on one PC is the ideal test. Friends can join later runs, but for this one keep it local.
  2. Respec each character into the build on its page (respec is 250 gold per level, 4,500 each at level 18; the purse has plenty). Point totals are exactly 20, the level-18 allowance, and tier unlocks are satisfied in order.
  3. Equip what the build says. Casters' damage comes from the weapon's damage number, so give each caster the hardest-hitting staff or wand you own.
  4. Config: stolenrealm.battlestats.cfg ships with LogEveryHit = true and WriteJson = true. Leave them. Verbose logging for the whole mod set is already on.
  5. Pick a fight with many enemies and at least one elite or champion, at or below level 18, any difficulty. A big open map is better than a corridor: the tiles need enemies walking through them. Torturous is fine and gives the enemies enough health to sit in poison for a few turns.
  6. Play the fight following the turn script (page 8). Take your time; there is no timing element.
  7. When the post-battle screen appears the summary is written. Then send BepInEx\LogOutput.log and the newest file in BepInEx\BattleStats\.

The battle, turn by turn

Order within a turn does not matter. The goal is that every path fires at least twice and that a few enemies stay alive long enough to take several ticks.

Before the first move

  • Note which enemies are where. Pick two enemies that will be kept alive as "tick sponges": poisoned, burning, bleeding and standing in tiles. Do not focus them.
  • Juggernaut takes the front; everyone else stays behind Brambles.

Turn 1: lay the ground

  • Ember: Burning Ground across the lane enemies will walk through.
  • Bloom: The Bad Bloom next to the largest group. Brambles between the group and the party.
  • Chaplain: Holy Ground under the party. Shield of Light on Juggernaut.
  • Bonecaller: Raise Skeletal Archer. Poison Cloud on the largest group.
  • Juggernaut: Rage, then Double Edge on the nearest enemy (watch for the self TAKEN line).
  • Cutthroat: Poison Weapon, then Dagger Throw at a tick sponge.

Turn 2: statuses and multi-target

  • Ember: Immolate a tick sponge. Fireball the group. Rune of Exploding under a cluster.
  • Bloom: Entangle the other sponge. Venomous Skin on Juggernaut. Nature Summoning II.
  • Chaplain: Regenerate on Juggernaut. Cure whoever took damage. Light's Beckon an enemy into Burning Ground.
  • Bonecaller: Haunt a sponge. Raise Skeletal Warrior. Tainted Touch anything.
  • Juggernaut: Bleeding Cleave into the group. Bash an enemy into a tile.
  • Cutthroat: Garrote a sponge. Basic attack something else (poison from the weapon).

Turn 3: the big shapes

  • Ember: Meteor on the group (direct hit plus lingering fire). Detonate if surrounded.
  • Bloom: Mass Entangle. The Good Bloom under the party.
  • Chaplain: Mass Cure. Shield of Retribution on Juggernaut, then let it break.
  • Bonecaller: Soul Link the two sponges. Consumption. Raise Skeletal Mage.
  • Juggernaut: Slam through a line. Basic attacks (Blood Drinker life steal).
  • Cutthroat: Smoke Bomb on the party. Deathblow on the lowest enemy: aim for a kill with overkill.

Turn 4 onward: let it run

  • Keep the sponges alive and inside the tiles; re-apply Immolate, Haunt, Entangle as they expire. Let the skeletons and the animal fight; try to let a summon take a kill.
  • Take a few hits on Juggernaut and on Bloom so Thorns, Fire Shield and Venomous Skin all fire.
  • Finish with Cutthroat and Juggernaut taking kills so both have kills in the summary. Then let the sponges die to ticks if you can: a KILL ... (Poisoned, StatusTick) line is the best possible evidence.
When the post-battle screen appears, open the stats tab and glance at it, then quit to the menu. The summary and the JSON are written the moment that screen opens.

Things worth writing down while you play

  • Any damage number you saw on screen that has no matching HIT line (who, roughly how much, what caused it).
  • Any line whose path label looks wrong to you: for example a Burning Ground tick labelled Direct, or a poison tick labelled with the wrong caster.
  • How Aura of Flame, Avatar of Flame, Colossus splash and Soul Link were labelled: those are the four we could not predict from the code.
